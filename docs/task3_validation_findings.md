## Gate behavior and clean-image tradeoff

On original validation, mean weights are approximately clean 0.379, salt 0.248, blur 0.168, occlusion 0.204. No branch is below 1% mean usage. Mild blur averages 59.2% identity plus 38.8% blur; severe blur averages 14.5% identity plus 84.8% blur. High-noise inputs average 99.9% salt expert. These condition-dependent patterns are consistent with preservation-oriented blending and do not show global branch collapse by the recorded usage checks.

Clean severity controls have mean identity weight 95.9%, SSIM 0.998884 and L1 0.000997 (about 0.254 of an 8-bit intensity level per channel on average). Median L1 is 0.000558, 95th percentile 0.002679, maximum 0.014217. One of 408 clean cases has PSNR below 30 dB; average preservation should not conceal outliers. Soft clean L1 is worse than hard clean L1, so this is a real tradeoff.

Overall original-validation PSNR falls from hard 49.15 to soft 34.93 dB largely because hard routing passes many clean images exactly (PSNR capped at 120), whereas the soft blend slightly changes them. Report per-condition scores and clean preservation, rather than treating this aggregate as evidence that corrupted restoration regressed.
